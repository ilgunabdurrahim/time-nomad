A DIFFERENT TIME ON EVERY FLOOR

From the Stone Age to today.

Your character jumps automatically. Drag left and right to guide the landing. As the eras change, so do the world, costume, platforms and music. Reach the next ledge before the rising mist catches you.

Journey

Explore 30 eras and find the six clock fragments. Checkpoints every five eras let you continue your journey; memories of the fragments reveal the tower’s story.

Record

Every attempt starts at the bottom. See how far you can climb in one run, with no continues. Your goal: beat your own best floor.

Clear rules. Simple play.

Eras and costumes unlock as you progress; no extra power is sold. Journey offers one optional ad-supported continue per run. Entering Record from the main menu is free. When Try again is labelled “with one ad”, at most one ad appears before that retry. Interstitials are at least 90 seconds apart. Results and leaderboards do not open ads.

A permanent Remove ads purchase also grants the same single continue without watching an ad. Store availability and pricing are shown in Google Play.
